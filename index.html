Our Philosophy

A Stone Worth a Lifetime

For three generations, jade has meant patience — the patience of mountains to form it, of masters to read it, and of a house willing to wait for the one stone worth cutting. Jade You continues that discipline: no mass production, no open shelf. Each piece is sourced, carved, and released to a single home, by invitation.

Provenance

From the Mountain to Your Hands

Four stages, each irreversible, each documented — the same path a piece of imperial jade has followed for centuries.

01

Sourced at Origin

Rough jadeite is hand-selected at source in Myanmar's Kachin region, long before it reveals what lies beneath its skin.

02

Read by the Master's Eye

Our jade masters study each stone's colour, water, and texture for weeks before a single cut is made.

03

Carved in the Atelier

Wheel-carved by hand in the old Cantonese tradition — a single piece may take months to complete.

04

Certified & Delivered

Independently certified by a gemological laboratory, then delivered privately by appointment or trusted courier.
